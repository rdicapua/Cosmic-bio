What It Is

The Concept
🌙
A Personal Clock
Enter your date of birth and three sine waves begin their count from that exact day. Every reading is unique to you — your birthday is the anchor from which all three cycles flow, never the same for any two people born on different days.
φ
Beautiful Mathematics
The three cycles are scaled by the most profound constants in mathematics: 1, the golden ratio φ, and π. Because φ and π are both irrational and incommensurate with each other, the three waves never simultaneously return to zero. Your pattern is, in the strictest mathematical sense, unrepeatable.
🔮
A Theatrical Oracle
Originally built for magic show performances, the app generates richly written predictions for any date — tonight, an anniversary, a first meeting. It also finds your next triple peak, when all three cycles crest simultaneously, and presents it as a personal prophecy worth circling on the calendar.

How It Works

Four Steps
I
Enter your date of birth
The app anchors all three cycles to the day you were born. Your birth date is the origin point — the moment from which lunar, golden, and transcendental time begins its count. Returning visitors are remembered; the app recalls the last reading automatically.
II
Read the three cycles
Three animated sine curves display your Emotional, Physical, and Intellectual cycles across a ±20-day window centered on tonight. Each curve shows a glowing dot at the present moment. Swipe left or right to travel through time; tap any date to jump directly to it.
III
Discover your fingerprint score
A six-decimal number — computed from the interference of all three cycles on this exact date — appears with a rarity rating. The mathematics guarantee it has never occurred before in precisely this form. A meter and a dramatic label complete the reveal.
IV
Receive the oracle & prophecy
Three richly written predictions, one per cycle, drawn from a rotating bank of theatrical texts. Below them, the app scans up to two years ahead and identifies the next date when all three cycles surge high simultaneously — your personal triple peak, presented as a prophecy worth marking on the calendar.
